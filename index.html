Current status · as of Nov 5

  • Lifted
    Most evacuation orders lifted Nov 3, 3:15 p.m.
    Homes on Hermosa St and San Juan St remain under mandatory evacuation. Stay away from riverbanks and barricades.3
  • Closed
    Hot Springs Blvd bridge
    Town says it is expected to reopen shortly (Nov 5). Nov 3 street closures: Hermosa, San Juan, N 5th, Hot Springs Blvd (US 160 to San Juan St), S 6th.3
  • Open
    US 160 First St bridge, 14th St, N 7th St
    First St bridge and 14th St reopened late Nov 2; N 7th St open Nov 4.3
  • Water
    Limit water use; avoid the river
    Sewer repairs continue with crews from Durango and Boulder. Residents asked to cut nonessential use (Nov 4). Sewage was discharged near the First St bridge.3
  • Damaged
    US 160 Piedra River bridge
    ~22 mi west; buckled with a section gone (last report Nov 2).5
  • Help

    Major disaster declaration requested
    FEMA damage assessment began Oct 27; no federal assistance approved as of Nov 5.3,7

Flood timeline

  1. Oct 22 – 26
    More than 6 in of rain across Archuleta County (town 5.2–5.8 in); 20 in of snow at Wolf Creek. October average is 2.27 in.4
  2. Fri Oct 23 · ~5:45 a.m.
    San Juan crests at 15.02 ft, second-highest on record. Piedra (10.48 ft), Navajo (7.8 ft) and Rio Blanco (5.56 ft) set records.4
  3. Tue Oct 27
    County reports ~800 structures affected, 540 of them homes. Town estimates ~$38M damage in town limits, ~$9M to sewer. No known deaths or injuries. FEMA damage assessment begins.4,3
  4. Sat Oct 31
    Gov. Polis requests a major disaster declaration for Archuleta and Rio Grande counties (~$27M initial public damage).7
  5. Sun Nov 1 · 11:56 a.m.
    "Evacuate now" for low-lying areas on the San Juan, Rio Blanco, Navajo and Piedra. Forecast crest raised to 21.84 ft. US 160 closed to inbound traffic at 2 p.m.5,6
  6. Sun Nov 1 · evening
    San Juan crests near 17 ft, below the forecast.1,6
  7. Mon Nov 2
    House strikes the Riverwalk bridge; museum bank erodes; Piedra US 160 bridge damaged; Aspen Springs cut off; cell and internet down. First St bridge reopens late evening.5,9
  8. Tue Nov 3
    Election Day at relocated voter centers.8
  9. Tue Nov 3 · 3:15 p.m.
    Most mandatory evacuation orders lifted; Hermosa St and San Juan St remain.3
  10. Wed Nov 4 – Thu Nov 5
    Water-use limits; sewer repairs with outside crews; Hot Springs Blvd bridge expected to reopen.3
